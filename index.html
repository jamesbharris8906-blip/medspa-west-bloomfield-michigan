Lasers & Skin Rejuvenation

Laser care can help with spots, redness, rough skin, and hair that keeps coming back. The first step is a close look at your skin in good light, plus a talk about sun, past burns, and what you hope to change. Some lasers work on color, some work on texture, and some work on hair. The team will say which one fits your skin, how it feels, and how your skin may look for a few days after. You will also get clear after-care, like sun care and gentle washing, so the work has a good chance to show.

Microneedling, Facials & Peels

This group covers the day-to-day work of skin care: deep cleans, light peels, and microneedling that helps skin make new collagen. A facial can clear pores and add water back to dry skin. A peel can help dull skin look brighter and smoother. Microneedling can help with marks left by acne, fine lines, and wide pores. The team picks the mix based on your skin that day, not a set menu. After a visit, you will know what to use, what to skip, and when to come back.

Injectables & PRF

Injectables cover Botox, Dysport, and fillers, while PRF uses a small amount of your own blood to help skin heal and look fuller. The talk before any needle is the most useful part. You will talk about where you want a soft change, where you want to leave things alone, and how long a result may last. PRF is a slower, natural-feeling choice for some people, and the team will say if it fits your goals. Small, careful steps tend to look best, and the team will tell you when a second visit makes sense.

Body Contouring & Wellness

XERF (Skin Tightening)

XERF is a newer choice for skin that has lost some of its spring. It does not use needles, and most people have no downtime after. The idea is simple: help your skin make more of its own collagen, elastin, and hyaluronic acid over time. The team can use it on the face, jaw, neck, and parts of the body. A talk first will cover which area bothers you most, what the visit feels like, and how long it may take for the change to show. Many people like that they can go back to a normal day right after.

Lip Fillers

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens at a first visit?

You talk first, then the team looks at your skin. Bring a list of what you use now and what has bothered you in the past. You will hear a few clear choices, what each one costs, and how much healing time it needs. You can book that day or think it over at home.

How often do people get Botox?

Many people come back every three to four months, but it is not the same for all. Your face, the dose, and how fast your body breaks it down all play a part. The team will note when your result starts to fade and help you pick a good time to return.

Are fillers safe?

Fillers are common, and a trained team talks about risks before any needle. You will hear about swelling, bruising, and rare issues, plus what to watch for after. Tell the team about allergies, medicines, and past filler visits so they can plan with care.

Can I get a facial before a wedding?

Yes, but timing matters. A light, hydrating facial a few days before can help skin look fresh. A strong peel or microneedling is better weeks before, so skin has time to calm. Tell the team your date, and they will plan a visit that fits it.

How long does laser hair removal take per visit?

Small areas, like the upper lip, can take a few minutes. Larger areas, like legs or a back, can take longer. Most people need a set of visits weeks apart, since hair grows in stages. The team will give you a time plan for the area you want treated.

What does XERF feel like?

Most people say it feels warm, with a mild tingle in the area being treated. There are no needles, and most people go back to normal plans right after. The team will talk you through each step, check how you feel, and adjust as needed during the visit.
